Privacy Policy for Project Desk — Personal PMO Suite

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Widiru Studio ("we", "our", or "developer") develops and maintains Project Desk — Personal PMO Suite ("the Extension"). We respect and are committed to protecting your privacy and data sovereignty.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Project Desk does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal or user data on external servers.

All project data, including Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) tasks, schedules, Gantt chart dependencies, Earned Value Management (EVM) metrics, risk registers, cost records, and preferences, are stored 100% locally on your computer using your browser's local storage APIs.

2. Permissions Justification

The Extension requests only the minimum necessary permissions strictly required for its core functionality:

  • storage & unlimitedStorage: Used exclusively to persist your project databases, tasks, and configuration locally on your device without hitting browser quota limits.
  • sidePanel: Used to open the Project Desk workspace inside Chrome's side panel, allowing you to manage projects alongside your active work tabs.
  • tabs: Used solely to allow you to launch Project Desk in a dedicated full-screen browser tab when requested by clicking the full-screen button.
  • contextMenus: Used to provide a convenient right-click shortcut to open the Project Desk workspace.

3. Third-Party Sharing and Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use user data for targeted advertising, creditworthiness assessment, or lending purposes. The Extension operates completely offline with zero telemetry or tracking scripts.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Extension does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
